How to use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Watch the counts update instantly as you type.
  3. Press Clear text to start again with a fresh document.

About this tool

Word limits are everywhere: school essays, meta descriptions, social posts, cover letters and text messages. This counter shows words, characters with and without spaces, sentences and paragraphs in one place so you can check a limit without guessing.

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, a common average for adult silent reading. Your text is processed only inside your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Any group of characters separated by spaces or line breaks counts as one word.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. Both totals are shown: with spaces and without.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything runs in your browser and disappears when you close the page.
