BMI Calculator

Calculate body mass index in metric or imperial units and see the healthy weight range for your height.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter your weight and height.
  3. Press Calculate BMI to see your category and healthy range.

About this tool

Body mass index compares weight with height and is widely used as a quick screening measure. The calculator applies the standard formula and the World Health Organization categories: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight, and 30 or above obese.

BMI is only a screening number. It does not measure body fat, muscle, age, sex or ethnicity, so athletes and older adults may get misleading results. Use it as a conversation starter with a healthcare professional, not as a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

Which formula is used?

Weight in kg divided by height in metres squared. For imperial units: 703 × pounds ÷ inches squared.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Muscle weighs more than fat, so muscular people can appear overweight.

Is this medical advice?

No. Please speak to a doctor for health decisions.
