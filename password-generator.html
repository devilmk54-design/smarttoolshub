Password Generator

Create strong, random passwords with your choice of length and character types.

How to use the Password Generator

  1. Move the slider to choose a length (16 or more is recommended).
  2. Tick the character types you want to include.
  3. Press Generate, then Copy and store the result in a password manager.

About this tool

Weak and reused passwords are the most common cause of hacked accounts. This tool builds each password with your browser’s cryptographically secure random number generator, the same source used for encryption keys, instead of predictable patterns.

A strength estimate is shown in bits of entropy. As a rule of thumb, 60 bits is fair, 80 is strong, and anything above 100 is excellent for important accounts. Passwords are created on your device and never sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to generate passwords online?

On this page, yes: generation happens entirely on your device and nothing is transmitted.

How long should a password be?

At least 14 to 16 characters for ordinary accounts, and longer for email and banking.

Should I include symbols?

Symbols raise strength, but length matters more. Some sites reject certain symbols, so untick them if needed.
