Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days, plus the days left until your next birthday.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Leave the second date as today, or choose another date to see your age on that day.
  3. Press Calculate age.

About this tool

Knowing an exact age matters for school admissions, visa forms, insurance, retirement planning and eligibility rules. This calculator uses calendar arithmetic, so month lengths and leap years are handled correctly.

It also shows the total number of days you have lived and how many days remain until your next birthday. People born on 29 February have their birthday counted as 1 March in non-leap years.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the “Age at date” field to any date on or after the birth date.

How are months and days worked out?

Complete years are counted first, then complete months, then the remaining days.

Is my birth date stored?

No. Nothing leaves your browser.
