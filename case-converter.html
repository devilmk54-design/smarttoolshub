Case Converter

Change text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case or kebab-case.

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Press the button for the format you want.
  3. Copy the result, or press Undo if you picked the wrong format.

About this tool

Fixing capitalisation by hand is slow and error-prone. Paste your text once and switch between seven formats with a single click, whether you are cleaning a headline, tidying a spreadsheet column or naming variables in code.

camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case are conventions programmers use for variable, file and URL names. The converter splits words on spaces, punctuation and capital letters, so myVariableName becomes my_variable_name correctly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises every word. Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence.

Does it keep line breaks?

Yes, the upper, lower, title and sentence options keep your line breaks.

Can I undo a conversion?

Yes. Undo restores the text from before the last conversion.
