How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Find the question that matches your problem.
  2. Type your two numbers into its boxes.
  3. Read the answer, which updates as you type.

About this tool

Percentages appear in discounts, tax, tips, grades, interest and business reports. Instead of remembering formulas, enter your numbers in the matching box and the answer appears instantly.

The three calculators cover finding X% of a value (a 15% tip on 80), working out what percent one number is of another (45 out of 60), and measuring increase or decrease between an old and a new value (a price rising from 50 to 65 is a 30% increase).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Use the first box: enter the discount percent and the original price to get the amount taken off.

What if the old value is zero?

Percentage change from zero is undefined, so the tool shows a message instead of a number.

Are negative results possible?

Yes. A negative change means a decrease.
