Color Converter

Convert colors between HEX, RGB and HSL with a live preview and color picker.

How to use the Color Converter

  1. Use the picker, or type a HEX, RGB or HSL value.
  2. The other formats and the preview update at once.
  3. Press Copy beside the format you need.

About this tool

Designers and developers constantly switch between color formats: HEX in CSS, RGB in graphics software, and HSL when adjusting lightness or saturation. Change any value here and the others update immediately.

HSL is often the easiest way to create variations: keep the hue and raise the lightness for a tint, or lower it for a shade. Press Copy next to any output to paste a ready-to-use CSS value.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between RGB and HSL?

RGB mixes red, green and blue light. HSL describes hue, saturation and lightness, which is closer to how people think about color.

Does it accept 3-digit HEX?

Yes. #f80 expands to #ff8800.

Why do values sometimes shift by 1?

Converting between formats involves rounding to whole numbers.
