Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information SmartToolsHub collects and how it is used.

Data you enter into our tools

All tools run locally in your web browser. The text, numbers and dates you type are processed on your device and are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps in server logs. This is used for security, performance and basic traffic statistics.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense or similar services to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

SmartToolsHub is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. Contact us through the contact page to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last revised.
