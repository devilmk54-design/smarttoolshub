Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, speed and temperature units instantly.

How to use the Unit Converter

  1. Choose a category such as length or weight.
  2. Enter a value and pick the units to convert from and to.
  3. Read the result, or press Swap to reverse the conversion.

About this tool

Recipes, travel, school science and online shopping often mix metric and imperial units. This converter handles everyday length, weight, speed and temperature units using exact conversion factors.

Every conversion goes through a base unit (metres, kilograms, metres per second, or Celsius) so results stay consistent in both directions.

Frequently asked questions

How precise are the results?

Results are shown to 10 significant digits.

How is temperature converted?

With formulas rather than a simple ratio, because Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin have different zero points.

Can I convert between categories?

No. You can only convert within one kind of measurement, such as length to length.
