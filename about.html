About Us

SmartToolsHub is a free website that brings together everyday online utilities in one clean place. We build tools for tasks people repeat often: counting words, creating passwords, converting units, checking a BMI, formatting data and more.

Our approach

We believe a tool should open instantly, do one job well and respect your privacy. Every tool on this site runs in your browser, so what you type stays on your device.

How we keep quality high

Each tool is tested with typical and edge-case inputs, and each page includes plain-language instructions and answers to common questions. If you find a mistake or have an idea for a new tool, please contact us.

How the site is funded

SmartToolsHub is free to use. We may display advertising in the future to cover hosting and development costs. Any ads will be clearly separate from tool results.
